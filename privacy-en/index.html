Privacy Policy

NOVAX apps (NOVAX QR, Habits+, H₂O NOVAX) · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Short and honest: NOVAX apps do not collect, transmit or store your personal data. Everything the apps do happens on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. The apps:

  • require no registration or account;
  • collect no personal data;
  • use no ads, analytics or trackers;
  • send no history, habits or scan data to external servers;
  • need no internet connection to work.

2. Permissions

The apps use only the standard Android permissions needed for their features, and only with your explicit action:

  • Camera (NOVAX QR) — only when you scan a code; images are processed on the device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Photos (NOVAX QR) — only when you choose "Scan from gallery"; the app reads the selected image locally to decode the code.
  • Notifications (Habits+, H₂O NOVAX) — optional; used only for the reminders you set yourself. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in system settings.

3. Your data on the device

Scan history, created QR codes, habits, reminders and water log are stored exclusively in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app removes this data.

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by the app store (Google Play). We never see or store your bank card details or payment information. Purchase status is verified with the store through its standard billing service.

5. Changes to this policy

The current version is always available on this page. If the rules change, we update the text here.
